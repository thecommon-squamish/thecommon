# The Common: original site editorial audit

Reviewed 2026-10-27 using the public WordPress pages and posts at thecommon.io. This is an editorial record for the replacement site, not a list of current offers.

## Peter's voice

- He speaks directly to visitors and members: what they need today, how to get in, what to do if something goes wrong.
- He is candid about building a community. The November 2017 member note says he had made a good space but had not done enough to bring members together.
- He names real things: lunch in the garden, a dog under a desk, the first drink at a social, Squamish people working in very different fields.
- He is practical and sometimes playful. The air hockey election event and April Fools outdoor offices belong to this character.
- The recurring idea is that working alongside people can improve daily life as well as work.

Copy rules for the new site: use we and you; choose concrete details over broad claims; keep booking instructions plain; keep the Squamish references accurate; avoid em dashes, generic slogans, and claims about current programs that have not been verified. Historical offers must carry dates or past-tense wording.

## Before replacing WordPress

- Preserve or redirect the old news URLs. The local story pages carry forward the first update and the community program details; other post summaries are on Our story.
- Confirm current availability of grants, sponsorship, private offices, membership benefits, and any hiring before adding a current-offer claim.
- Keep OfficeRnD as the booking source. Historical room names and old pricing are not copied into current booking paths.
